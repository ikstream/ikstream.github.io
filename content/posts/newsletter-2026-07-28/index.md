An interesting thought to design platforms to be safe not just for children but for people in general, with child protection added on top. The focus here is on doom scrolling, endless notifications, fake news, and so on. Things we consider unsuitable for children because they influence them also quite clearly influence adults.
- https://www.impactgrid.org/post/from-child-safe-to-human-safe-why-digital-platforms-need-universal-design-standards

Google is updating its naming schema for threat actors. The new schema consists of two words, where the first is a unique, memorable name and the second reflects attribution, motivation, or activity. Other factors may also play a role.
- https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/updated-cyber-threat-actor-naming-system/
