Google's Threat Intelligence Group looked at statistics on published and exploited vulnerabilities. In August of this year alone, over 10,000 vulnerabilities were published, a significant increase compared to January. Exploitation has risen at roughly the same rate. Also included is a breakdown of the ratio between AI-discovered and human-discovered vulnerabilities.
- https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/vulnerability-discovery-and-exploitation-trends-in-the-ai-era/

MS Copilot is now available across a variety of systems, and that brings new attack surface with it. Case in point, Microsoft's Copilot in SSMS (SQL Server Management Studio). At BlueHat Asia, Johann Rehberger presented CVE-2026-65669, a SQL Server Elevation of Privilege vulnerability that Microsoft rated as critical.
- https://embracethered.com/blog/posts/2026/from-select-to-sysadmin-sql-copilot-bluehat-asia/
