Another Week, another round. 

---
Google Threat Intelligence Group looked at the LLMjacking market. It covers the trade in initial access data on compromised infrastructure, but also the practice of mixing access into proxy gateways for use. Leaked or stolen cloud access tokens were the most common initial access vector.
- https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/llmjacking-monetizing-compromised-cloud-models/

---
Rasmus Moorats ran into trouble after the clock change when trying to set the time on his 2014 Renault. His only option was to reverse engineer the firmware of the dashboard and take a closer look at the whole thing.
- https://blog.nns.ee/2026/11/02/renault-hack/

---
A single attacker ran attacks against targets in 11 countries using 12 agents. According to Acronis TRU, the initial models refused to carry out direct attacks, so the attacker had to fall back on local models without restrictions. Those models caused quite a few mistakes, but there is still plenty to learn from them.
- https://www.acronis.com/en/tru/posts/bossai-inside-the-ai-attack-mesh-that-targeted-taiwans-emergency-services/
