Another Week, another round. 

---

Genians' Security Center published an analysis of a new threat actor framework whose TTPs show similarities to known North Korean threat actors. It uses a multi-stage chain with LNK files and PowerShell. Instructions are stored and retrieved via aNotebook, a public notebook service, and exfiltration happens through Dropbox.
- https://www.genians.co.kr/en/blog/threat_intelligence/nodelinkrat

---
Hudson Rock Research introduces a new service from a cybercrime forum that turns the offered access into searchable profiles. That makes targeted purchasing of accesses for one's own purposes considerably more effective.
- https://www.infostealers.com/article/inside-the-ai-powered-marketplace-turning-infected-computers-into-searchable-profiles/

---
I've always liked VLC, even though it's been a while since I last used it. He Wei / Gikaku presents a CVE in which a 711-byte MP4 gets past the parser that was refactored in 2020 and reaches an overflow vulnerability that was actually meant to be unreachable.
- https://gikaku.net/2026/11/17/vlc-mp4-rtp-hint-type-confusion/

---
Along with the last newsletter, a new issue of Phrack has come out with many interesting articles.
- https://phrack.org/issues/73/abusing-the-windows-smart-card-minidriver-architecture_md#article

---
Talos suspects with low confidence that UAT-12643 is a threat actor linked to North Korea, and describes the infrastructure and techniques it uses. The actor relies on Overlord RAT, LNKs, LOLBins, and an HTADropper.
- https://blog.talosintelligence.com/uat-12643-suspected-north-korea-aligned-actor-blends-blockchain-resilience-with-targeted-infostealing/

---
And since gaming keeps coming up again and again, we're just going to keep going with it.
- https://trustedsec.com/blog/hacking-the-my-arcade-contra-pocket-player-part-ii

---
Dbgman shows that there is a 9-second window between the initialization and the activation of the EDR kernel driver, in which it is possible to overwrite relevant registers and effectively disable the EDR.
- https://0xdbgman.github.io/posts/the-9-second-window/
